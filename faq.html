Frequently Asked Questions

How Does PilotRep Calculate Reputation?

PilotRep calculates reputation by averaging all the grades a pilot receives from other players. When leaving a Rep, users select a letter grade from F to A+. Each grade carries an underlying numeric value used to compute the overall score.

Consistent high grades trend toward A or A+ while mixed grades settle in the middle. A single bad grade won't tank a profile, but a pattern of low grades will impact the score over time. Every rep carries equal weight in the current calculation.

Can I Submit An Anonymous Rep?

Yes. While logged in and leaving a rep, you can choose to attribute it to your authenticated character or submit it anonymously.

Is PilotRep Affiliated With Fenris Creations?

No. PilotRep is an independent, third-party tool built for the EVE Online community.

Can I Rate Corporations Or Alliances, Or Only Individual Pilots?

You can search and view profiles for Pilots, Corporations and Alliances across New Eden, as well as leave and view Reps for all three.

Can I Edit Or Delete A Rep I've Already Submitted?

No. All Reps are securely and permanently stored on the PilotRep servers to maintain the integrity of the rating system. If a submission contains an error or violates our Terms of Service, please reach out via our Contact Form.

How Does PilotRep Prevent Review Bombing And Spam?

We employ several layered measures to protect the platform from vote manipulation and abuse:

Required Authentication

Pilots must be logged in via official EVE Online SSO to leave a Rep.

Rate Limits

Players are restricted on how frequently they can rate the same pilot.

Affiliation Separation

Reps left by corporation and alliance mates are displayed separately from general community reviews to ensure transparency and highlight potential bias.

Content Constraints

Comments are capped by word and character limits to keep feedback concise and prevent walls of text.

Active Moderation and Filters

Automated profanity filters block abusive language, and comments are monitored for policy violations.

Do I Need An Account To Use PilotRep?

No. You can search and view any pilot, corporation or alliance without logging in. However, to leave a Rep or use the advanced Search you must log in with your EVE Online account.

How Is My Character Verified?

PilotRep uses the official EVE Single Sign-On (SSO) system to verify character ownership. The authentication happens entirely through the secure SSO portal, meaning PilotRep never sees or stores your password or account credentials.

How To Verify Your Character

Click "Log In with EVE Online"

You will be redirected to the secure authentication portal.

Select Your Character

Choose the specific character on your account you wish to authenticate with.

Review Permissions

PilotRep only requests minimal public-info scopes required to verify identity.

Approve And Return

Once approved, you are redirected back to PilotRep fully authenticated.

Can Other Players See My Data?

Yes, standard public data points — including Character Name, Character ID, Corporation/Alliance affiliations, Security Status, Character Creation Date and Portrait — are visible on public profiles.

Where Does PilotRep Get Its Data?

Data is pulled directly from public EVE Online endpoints via the ESI API, combined with verified player Reps and Votes.

How Often Is Reputation Updated?

Reps are updated instantly upon submission.
