Reputation Precedes You

In a universe where trust is currency and betrayal is a profession, PilotRep serves the Pilots, Corporations and Alliances of New Eden by letting you know exactly who is flying next to you.

We provide the EVE Online community with a neutral and structured space to rate their encounters. From reliable logistics runners and mercenary outfits to scammers, gankers, thieves and stalwart allies, every Rep left is a data point in a living record of New Eden's social fabric.

Built for Pilots by a Pilot

I was drawn into New Eden after seeing the iconic "This is EVE" trailer, and have never looked back. Over the years I've operated across multiple accounts and characters experiencing nearly every facet of New Eden.

The Explorer

Driven by the thrill of uncharted space, covert cloaks, silent scanning and cracking hidden relic anomalies.

The Line Member & Operative

Experienced in everything from tight-knit small-gang null-sec ops to massive mega-corporations, with a deep-seated love for stealth and Black Ops operations.

Having flown in nearly every sector of space, and having experienced both incredible camaraderie and devastating betrayals, I built PilotRep to give Pilots a tool that actually matters when making split-second decisions on who to trust.

How PilotRep Works

SSO Verified Authenticity

Every review is tied to a verified character via EVE Online Single Sign-On (SSO).

Reputation At Scale

Search, review and track reputation metrics across individual Pilots, Corporations and Alliances.

Living Social Metrics

Track real-time interaction feeds, view top-reputed pilots across the cluster and inspect detailed encounter notes and comments.

Community Driven Reputation

A structured platform designed to record history as it happens, whether you're vetting a new corp recruit, verifying a contractor or checking who just entered local. All reps are permanent, creating a lasting and trustworthy record for the community.
